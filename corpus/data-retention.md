# Data Retention

Support conversation transcripts are retained for twenty-four months and then
deleted permanently.

Billing records are retained for seven years to satisfy financial reporting
obligations. They cannot be deleted on request before that period elapses.

A customer may request deletion of their personal data at any time. Deletion
requests are completed within thirty days, except for records covered by the
billing retention obligation above.

Audit logs of administrative actions are retained for thirteen months. Audit
logs are append-only and cannot be edited by any role, including
administrators.
