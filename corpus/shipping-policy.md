# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping is dispatched within two business days of the order being
placed and arrives within five to eight business days.

Express shipping is dispatched the same business day for orders placed before
14:00 local time and arrives within one to two business days.

International shipping is available to most destinations. Customs duties and
import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in
the shipping charge quoted at checkout.

A shipment that has not arrived fourteen days after dispatch is treated as
lost. Lost shipments are replaced at no cost once the carrier has confirmed
the loss.
