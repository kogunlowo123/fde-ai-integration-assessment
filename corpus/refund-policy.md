# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically settle within
five to seven business days.

A refund requires a written reason of at least ten characters so that the
support team can audit why money was returned. Refunds above 500 units of
currency require approval from a supervisor before they are issued.

Refunds are not available for accounts in a suspended state. A suspended
account must be reinstated by the account security team before any refund can
be processed.

Partial refunds are permitted for subscription products. The refunded amount
is prorated by the number of unused days remaining in the billing period.
